    Help and FAQs

    How do I set my budget?

    Enter your monthly spending target in the budget field and select Update budget. The summary will recalculate automatically.

    How do I add an expense?

    Enter a name, amount, category and date. Select Add expense to include it in your spending total.

    What happens if I go over budget?

    The amount left will become negative and the progress bar will change color so you can see that spending is over your target.

    Can I filter my expenses?

    Use the category filter above your expense list to show only expenses from one category.

    Can I delete an expense?

    Select Delete next to an expense to remove it. Clear all removes every expense from the current page session.
